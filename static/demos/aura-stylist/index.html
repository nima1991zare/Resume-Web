Dressing an hourglass shape
  • Define the waist — wrap dresses, belted coats and high-rise bottoms are your allies.
  • Choose fitted or softly draped fabrics; boxy cuts hide your natural balance.
  • V-necks and scoop necklines keep proportions elegant up top.
  • Straight and bootcut trousers mirror your shoulders-to-hips symmetry.
